## Jo

### Walk or run by default

Jo's default ground speed is 280 px/s. At 1 m = 44 px that is 6.4 m/s, which is a run, so the walk cycle only ever showed during the first frame of a start. That is what the owner saw.

**Choice: the default stays the run, and holding Shift (gamepad LB, action `walk`) walks at 80 px/s.**
- 80 px/s is 1.8 m/s, a brisk human walk.
- Every dream's jumps, gaps and chase timings are tuned to 280. A slower default would change gameplay everywhere, and the brief says gameplay must stay identical.
- A real walk cannot be played at 280 px/s: the legs would cycle at about 35 fps.
- So the normal movement is now drawn as an honest run (lean and pumping arms, unchanged), and the walk is a real, separate gait you can choose.
- If the owner wants a walk by default in calm places (the hub, the Evening), that is a per-scene `player.speed` change. It needs the hub and the Evening re-timed, so it is left to the owner.

### What changed

| Animation | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Walk | Jo's old table (step 3, contacts ±6) | Generated by `walkCycle`: upright torso (no lean), heel-to-toe step 4 (one leg-length), arms hanging low and nearly straight, ±1 hip bob with the head a frame late. Contacts move exactly 4 px a frame. |
| `run_start` | 3 frames on the clock, while Jo accelerated (the push-off slid) | 4 frames, stride-locked (step 4): the weight drops, the back leg drives, the arms throw. It ends after the ground is covered, or after 260 ms against a wall. |
| `run_stop` (skid) | 4 frames | 5 frames: heel digs in, body sits back, arms fly forward, then he straightens. Dust on frames 0 and 1. |
| Turn | 3 frames | 4 frames: momentum carries him onto the planted foot, the head comes round first, feet cross, then he drives off. Dust on frame 0. |

Thresholds are unchanged: walk below 110 px/s, jog 110–240, run above 240. Shift sets `walkOnly`, so no `run_start` plays into a walk.

Sheets: `sheets/jo-walk-vs-run.png` (walk, jog, run) and `sheets/jo-start-stop-turn.png`.

## Gaits for everyone else

`src/art/rig/gaits.js` holds the gaits.
- A walk generator places the planted foot exactly `step` px further back each frame, for any frame count and any body.
- The run uses the four key poses.
- `tests/characters.test.js` checks every gait on every body (adult m and f, big, elderly, teen, child).

The cast bake now writes, for each person:
- the gait walk (`key#1..N`)
- a run (`key-run#1..8`) for foes, the musician and astronaut NPCs, and Paul
- an idle loop (`key-idle#1..8`): a signature idle for foes, a task loop for NPCs
- a 3-frame alert, `search` ×4 and `stagger` ×2
- `index.json.gaits[key]` = `{ walk: [frames, px], run, runAbove, idle }`

### Foe behaviour

- The 3-frame notice: the head snaps round, the brow drops, a red glint, then a point.
- On the first sight of Jo the foe now **stops for 0.4 s** (skill §6). This is the only gameplay-visible change, and it gives the player time to react.
- The 3-frame windup ends with both arms up high, so the 0.4 s tell reads.
- A shoved foe shows `stagger` instead of walking backwards.
- A distracted foe plays `search`.

## No sliding: every moving character

`src/art/stride.js` covers every case below:
- `Stride` plays legs from the ground actually covered (`dx`), with walk/run hysteresis.
- Moving against the facing direction plays the cycle backwards.
- Standing still shows the idle loop, never a walk frame.
- `attachStride(scene, img, key)` watches any image's `x`.

| Mover | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Human foes (all dreams) | Jo's walk at 6 px a frame (big 7), fixed 0.016 s step, so leg speed depended on frame rate; one idle frame | Own gait from real `dx` and real delta; run above the gait's threshold; signature idle |
| Creature foes | Fixed 0.016 s step | Real delta |
| Gambler chip runner (lunges on a tween) | Idle frame while lunging | Legs from real `dx` |
| The Pit (140 px/s) | Idle frame plus 2 walk frames at 22 px (slid) | Run frames, locked |
| Evening Townsfolk and extras | Stepped every 6 px (kids 3.5) whatever the body, so elderly, child and big bodies slipped | Gait from real `dx`; kids on 6 frames; task-loop idle; no steps while riding the bike |
| Evening companion holding hands | `p.walkFrame` was undefined, so she slid on one frame | Legs from her own `dx` |
| Gambler, Writer and Painter `walkPerson` | 6 px, 8 frames for every body | `Stride` per person |
| Paul (Painter follower, 140–220 px/s) | 14 px a frame on a 6 px walk (slid) | Walk, then run frames, locked |
| Hub travellers (Silhouette, scaled 0.78–0.94) | Unscaled 6 px plus a second hip bob | Step × scale, the rig's own bob |
| Musician: scalper, Nia, pedestrians, the kid, Marcus on the road, roadies | One frame while moving | `attachStride` |
| Astronaut: Priya following, the boxing opponent | One frame while moving | `attachStride`; backing off plays the cycle backwards |
| Jo walking into and out of the train (`Train.js`) | 6 px a frame | Jo's manifest step × scale (8 px) |
