# THE MASTER'S LADDER (platform 5)

The painter's dream, rebuilt. It replaces THE YELLOW HOUSE (`docs/dream_painter_the_yellow_house.md`). The old scene stays playable behind `?legacy=1` and `?warp=painter_legacy`.

The owner asked for "an environment like the Renaissance, when artists were encouraged. How painters reached up the ladder in that era. Maybe at the end we paint something famous, or compete with someone famous." Every name here is invented. Famous works are described by their kind, never by title.

## Whose dream it is
- **The dreamer.** An old woman rides the train to platform 5 with gold leaf under her nails: **Bice**. In waking life she restored church walls for fifty years and never signed one.
- **Her dream.** It is the city of **Fioralba** in the spring the new chapel was put to a contest. In it she is sixteen, the master's daughter. She draws better than anyone in the workshop, and the guild will not take a girl.
- **Jo.** Jo is the new errand boy, the hands she was never allowed. He climbs the whole ladder of the trade in one day: errand boy, apprentice, assistant, guild master, commissioned painter.
- **The finale.** At the top of the scaffold, the drawing he paints from turns out to be hers. He paints the vault with her.

## The finale: the contest of the vault
- **The contest.** It is modelled on the city contests of that age, where two masters competed for a door panel, a dome or a chapel. Here it is the two bays of the new chapel's vault, each in **one giornata**: the plaster must be painted while it is wet.
- **The rival.** Maestro **Anselmo Dori**, the celebrated master, works the left bay. His progress fills a bar.
- **Jo and Bice paint the right bay.** It is a night sky of ultramarine sown with gilded stars, with a figure in a blue mantle at its centre: the kind of vault every chapel of that age wanted.
- **The steps:**
  1. pounce the cartoon (charcoal through the pricked holes)
  2. the sky
  3. the stars in gold leaf
  4. the mantle
  5. the fold
  6. the face, which Bice paints and Jo holds the ladder for
- **The clock.** The **drying plaster** is the clock. If it dries, that patch is chipped off and laid fresh, and the work goes a little slower. The work is patient and is always finished.
- **The judging.** The bell rings and the scaffolds come down. The city chooses. The ending lines follow how cleanly you painted and whether you named Bice.

## Foes and obstacles of the era
- **Rival apprentices** (the Dori workshop, green caps; a skulking gait): they grab and throw Jo out of their street.
- **Guild inspectors** (red robes, tall hats; a measured, hands-behind-the-back gait): the guild hall and the square.
- **The jealous foreman Bruno** (big, rope coil; a heavy gait): the chapel scaffold, where he guards the hidden cartoon.
- **Scaffolding.** Plank gaps and ladders. Pigeons in the vault hurt on contact and can be stomped.
- **Drying plaster:** the clock in both the `drapery` gesso and the finale's giornata.
- **The verb [Q] SKETCH.** Jo draws in silverpoint. People stop to look (foes are distracted), and three **studies** around the city (the antique torso by the fountain, the dome's centering, a beggar's hands) are optional, worth a line in the ending.

## Collectibles (collectibles skill)
- **Coin: the giglio**, a small gold lily coin (invented), **worth 3, budget 80–100**. Every cluster sits on the harder way: the loggia roof, the washing lines, the jar shelves, the dome-side scaffold. There are breadcrumbs only at decision points.
- **Key items**, each on a lit pedestal behind its place's main challenge, and each opening one gate:
  - **LAPIS**: the colour-seller's top shelf, past a rival apprentice. It opens the drapery.
  - **GUILD SEAL**: won with the masterpiece. It opens the palazzo.
  - **GOLD-LEAF BOOK**: the palazzo's study, past the foreman's men. It opens the chapel, together with the commission.
  - **CARTOON**: the chapel's mid scaffold, where Bruno hid it. It opens the vault.
- **Heart shard:** above the colour-seller's alembic, on the hardest shelf run.
- **Small Moment "Bice's hand":** the bottega's back bench, after the drapery. Jo watches her draw a hand from memory.
